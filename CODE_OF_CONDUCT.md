# Code of conduct

## Our standard

DevDock is a small open-source project. Everyone involved — maintainers, contributors, and people opening issues — is expected to treat others with respect and to assume good faith.

Behavior that helps the project:

- Be direct about technical problems and kind about people.
- Accept that reviewers may disagree with an approach, and explain reasoning rather than repeating assertions.
- Welcome questions from people new to the project, the tooling, or the language.
- Credit others' work and prior discussion.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private, including unwelcome attention after a request to stop.
- Discriminatory language or jokes, including those about age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit history — and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately through the repository host's private reporting feature, the same channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled as discreetly as the situation allows. Include only what is needed to understand what happened; do not include other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying this standard and may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that conflict with it. Depending on severity and history, a response may be a private warning, removal of the contribution, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face the same consequences.

This document is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), simplified for a project of this size.
